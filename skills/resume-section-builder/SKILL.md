# Resume Section Builder


Honor the requested layout and section order when compatible with readable output. Use ordinary headings and a clear reading flow; do not turn these defaults into universal hiring rules.

- Contact: keep essential information in the main body and validate URLs. Respect a requested single line if it fits comfortably.
- Summary: write after selecting achievements. Establish role, relevant scope and strongest supported outcomes; avoid copying every metric again into a context paragraph.
- Skills: organize concise groups, preserving the user's requested canonical shared block across versions. Keep weak/experimental exposure distinct when relevant; do not add a posting's technologies without evidence.
- Employment: show employer, actual role and dates clearly, including promotions and contract periods. Grouped promotions may be interpreted differently by parsers; inspect the extracted chronology instead of inventing extra dated jobs.
- Cross-project work: clearly identify its span, or integrate it where dates are established. Avoid an undated pseudo-employer subsection.
- Projects: label personal projects, demos and production deployments accurately. Preserve portfolio links without implying paying customer adoption.
- Education: retain the verified degrees and institutions; do not infer missing dates or credentials.
- Length: select evidence and remove repetition before adjusting typography. Use readable type, useful whitespace and deliberate page boundaries.

Return the proposed section structure and targeted edits. Do not remove older work or education solely because it is less recent. Coordinate artifact formatting with resume-document-delivery.
