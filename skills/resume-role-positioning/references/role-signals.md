# Role signals

These are practical editorial lenses developed for this workflow, not claims about every employer.

## Senior software engineering
Lead with hands-on design and implementation, system behavior under load, data/API contracts, reliability, testing and operations. Show end-to-end ownership and leverage through mentoring or design reviews. Identify constraints and technical tradeoffs rather than listing services launched.

## Staff software engineering
Use distinct evidence of technical direction across teams, reusable platform adoption, architecture strategy, resolving ambiguity and disagreement, migrations spanning ownership boundaries, and durable organizational improvements. Explain the mechanism of influence and continued outcome. A senior title or team coordination alone does not establish staff scope. Keep official titles intact.

## AI engineer: software engineering / applied AI
Lead with software shipped around models: orchestration, tools, retrieval/data grounding, evaluation datasets and methods, observed quality, tracing/debugging, fallback behavior, permissions where relevant, latency/cost tradeoffs and production operations. Ask which parts the candidate owned. Distinguish applied LLM systems from model research, training, fine-tuning and ML infrastructure. Only claim those when supported.

## Forward deployed engineering
Lead with discovering ambiguous user/customer problems, agreeing success criteria, writing working integrations/software, adapting to real data and environments, iterating through demos/pilots, rollout, adoption and support. Show engineering depth alongside communication. Describe internal business partner deployments truthfully as internal; describe independent prototypes as prototypes. External customer, revenue and travel claims require evidence.
